# Hypothesis Generator Prompt Contract

Not used by deterministic Phase 3 generation. Reserved for evidence gaps that later justify AI.

- Role: generate specific, testable, evidence-linked attack hypotheses.
- Inputs: endpoint inventory, model, and invariants.
- Mutation dimensions: actor, object, state, time, value, channel, version.
- Output: schema-validated hypotheses with evidence provenance, transparent scores, and safety notes.
- Failure condition: reject generic advice such as "test for IDOR."
